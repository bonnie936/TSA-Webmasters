In Order to Use AI Ethically, You Have To:

Check your organization’s AI policy

  • Many different schools, scholarships, and companies have their own AI use policies. It’s important to check these policies before using AI for any of your work to ensure you aren’t violating their standards.

Use it as an assistant, not a replacement

  • Use AI to assist you in your learning, not just to get answers so you can submit a specific assignment. You should still engage in an authentic learning environment, only using AI when you truly don’t understand a concept or when you want to brainstorm specific topics.

Respect copyright and consent

  • Be familiar with copyright and consent policies when generating or altering images with AI. Do not use generative AI to mimic a real person’s voice, face, or likeness without their permission. Additionally, if you do use AI in your school, workplace, or personal life, it is important to make this known.
