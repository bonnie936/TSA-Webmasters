Practically Using AI

What does each tool specialize in?

To use AI efficiently, you first must know what each tool specializes in. This is important for you to get the best results from each tool. For instance...

  • OpenAI's ChatGPT is the best all-around choice for web search and brainstorming
  • Anthropic's Claude is strongest for writing, editing, creative work, and coding

Be specific and clear in your prompt: Aim, Context, Rules

  • Aim: What do you want the AI to do?
  • Context: Who is this for and what background does it need?
  • Rules: Any limits on length, tone, or format. This saves you time from fussing with the AI later on when it does not give you what you exactly need or want.

Fact Check

  • Remember, AI predicts likely text rather than verifying facts, so it can sound confident and still be wrong. Check what it generates before you use it.
