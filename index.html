Utilizing tools effectively and ethically

How to Use AI as a Tool

AI can be used as a tool in many ways. However, it is important to use it in ways that benefit you. As a student, AI can be helpful to get assignments done, learn how to solve problems, and study new subjects whenever you have a subpar teacher. However, it’s important to conduct your use of AI in an ethical way. You can do this by first solving the problems on your own. You can only learn if you first attempt something by yourself. Furthermore, once you’ve tried one problem using the help of AI, you should keep practicing and try the rest on your own. Only use AI if you get stuck and a teacher isn’t readily available.
